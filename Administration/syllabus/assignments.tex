\section*{Homework Assignments}
The course's homework assignments provide 
opportunities for deeper practice of 
object-oriented software development skills introduced in class. 
To get the greatest learning benefit, 
you should start assignments early, 
seek help when you get stuck, and 
review feedback on your submissions. 

Unless an assignment's instructions 
explicitly allow/require the use of 
genAI tools or other resources, 
you should only use class resources and the Java {API}. 
Any time you get help while working on an assignment, 
whether from an instructor, classmate, TA, 
Learning Center tutor, or an online resource, 
you should cite the help near the top of your submitted file{(s)}. 

Homework assignments are for practice, 
so your final grade depends not on perfect accuracy in homework submissions 
but on overall completion thresholds, 
where \emph{completion} requires making an honest effort and, if applicable, 
passing nearly all unit tests. 
See individual assignment instructions for more details. 

Assignments will be submitted through Gradescope, 
which you can access by clicking the assignment links in Moodle. 
You can access your grades in Moodle 
and feedback in Gradescope. 
If you believe there has been an error, 
please contact your instructor.

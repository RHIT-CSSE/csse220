\section*{Institute Policies}
\subsection*{Students with Accessibility Needs:}
Rose-Hulman is committed to working with students 
who have special needs or disabilities. 
Visit the \href{https://rosehulman.sharepoint.com/sites/sas/SitePages/Home.aspx}{Accessibility Services website}
for more information.  
Requests for academic accommodations must be 
documented with and approved by the Accessibility Services office 
before use in this course. 
\textbf{If you have difficulty accessing any course materials, 
please let your instructor know.}

\subsection*{Student Counseling:} 
The \href{https://rosehulman.sharepoint.com/sites/counseling/SitePages/Home.aspx}{Student Counseling Center} 
helps students identify barriers to success, 
build personal awareness, and develop life skills.  
Counseling appointments are free and confidential. 

\subsection*{Academic Integrity:} 
The \href{https://www.rose-hulman.edu/campus-life/student-life/_assets/Student-Handbook.pdf}{Student Handbook} 
and Rose-Hulman's \href{https://www.rose-hulman.edu/campus-life/student-services/registrar/rules-and-procedures/index.html}{Academic Rules and Procedures} 
describe penalties and processes invoked as a consequence 
if academic misconduct (such as cheating, plagiarizing, 
or interfering with the academic progress of other students) takes place. 
It is the responsibility of each student to know and follow Rose-Hulman's rules about academic integrity. 
See the Academic Integrity section in this syllabus for course-specific policies. 

\subsection*{Community and Belonging:}
As an institution devoted to excellence and innovation in education, 
we acknowledge our responsibility to create 
opportunities for growth and development, 
including the ability to interact with people 
of all backgrounds, experiences, and viewpoints. 
The \href{https://www.rose-hulman.edu/campus-life/student-services/office-community-belonging/index.html}{Office of Community and Belonging} 
seeks to advance a sense of belonging on campus where 
each individual is valued and accepted for 
their unique experiences, backgrounds, and aptitudes. 

\subsection*{Online Access Requirements:} 
Rose-Hulman welcomes students from around the world, 
and encourages faculty, staff and students to travel around the world.  
However, geopolitical conditions and compliance with export law and regulations 
prevent us from delivering certain kinds of educational experiences 
and/or using certain kinds of Institute technologies in some locations. 
For example, students in locations with limited access to the internet in general, 
with restricted access to portions of the internet, 
or which are embargoed or sanctioned by the United States government 
may not be able to successfully complete Rose-Hulman courses.

\subsection*{Emergency Information:} 
To receive email or text messages regarding 
emergency situations that may impact campus and, 
possibly, the delivery of classes, 
\href{https://www.getrave.com/login/rose-hulman}{register for RAVE alerts} 
and/or follow \href{https://twitter.com/RoseHulmanAlert}{@RoseHulmanAlert}. 
Any announcements about the Institute's ability to offer classes 
will be shared on Rose-Hulman's website. 

\subsection*{Student Well-Being and Academic Success:}
Rose-Hulman cares about the well-being of every student. 
Stress is a normal part of the college experience 
and is often compounded both by classwork and 
unexpected challenges outside the classroom. 
I encourage you to reflect on how you take care of yourself throughout the term, 
especially before the demands of exams and projects reach their peak.  
You can reach out to the 
\href{https://rosehulman.sharepoint.com/sites/LC/SitePages/Home.aspx}{Learning Center}, 
\href{https://rosehulman.sharepoint.com/sites/osas/SitePages/Home.aspx}{Student Academic Success}, or 
the \href{https://rosehulman.sharepoint.com/sites/counseling/SitePages/Home.aspx}{Student Counseling Center} 
(which offers both online and in-person resources) if you would like assistance. 
Additional resources to help maintain your work-life balance can be found on 
the \href{https://www.rose-hulman.edu/campus-life/index.html}{Campus Life} web page 
and within the \href{https://library.rose-hulman.edu/StudentSuccess}{Student Success Guide}. 
If you, or someone you know, is struggling, 
you can let us know through the 
\href{https://rosehulman.sharepoint.com/sites/Rosecares/SitePages/Home.aspx}{Rose Cares} 
system, and someone will reach out.
